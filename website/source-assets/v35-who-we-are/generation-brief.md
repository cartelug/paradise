# Generation direction

These summarize the generation instructions from the conversation; the two
approved PNG masters, rather than a regenerated interpretation, are authoritative.

## Desktop

Single flat desktop Who We Are web section for Pardus Luxury Escapes, 3:2.
Luminous ivory, aqua, turquoise and cobalt gradients organically blend a vivid
Positano-inspired coastline, Seychelles granite lagoon and golden African
savannah, with a walking leopard. No framed photos, arch, cards or book.
The typography is the designed artwork: large navy editorial serif headline
“A world of places.” with cobalt italic “places.”; gold italic secondary phrase
“A journey of your own.”. Keep body copy plain, clear and highly readable.

Exact body: “We are Pardus Luxury Escapes.” and “We arrange flights, stays,
safaris and private experiences across Africa and around the world.”
Button: “Discover Pardus”. Section label: “02 / WHO WE ARE”. Brand label:
“PARDUS LUXURY ESCAPES”. Bottom service labels: “FLIGHTS”, “STAYS”, “SAFARIS”,
“PRIVATE EXPERIENCES”, with fine navy travel symbols. A fine gold route and
small plane connect the scenes. Photographic textures, bright natural light,
restrained detail, no fabricated claims or awards.

## Mobile

Use the approved desktop as reference. Redesign the composition for a narrow
390-CSS-pixel phone, approximately 9:20 portrait. Preserve the same scene,
colour system and typography. Headline wraps to “A world” / “of places.”;
secondary phrase “A journey of” / “your own.”. Place body and CTA on the
upper light wash, coast to the right, lagoon and leopard below. Keep the
entire walking animal visible. Recompose the bottom services as a 2×2 grid.
No device frame, horizontal overflow, new copy or unapproved claims.
