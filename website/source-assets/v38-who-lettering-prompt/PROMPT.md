# Who We Are heading: regeneration prompts (V38)

For the owner to run in an image generator. Wording is unchanged (“A world of
places.” / “A journey of your own.”); only the craft of the lettering is new.
Attach the current `who-lettering-master.png` (desktop) or
`who-lettering-mobile-master.png` (phone) as the reference for wording and palette.
Export as a transparent PNG; the site pipeline is
`node scripts/prepare-illustrated-home.mjs --art=who-lettering` (desktop) and
`--art=who-lettering-mobile` (phone).

## Desktop (about 3.6 : 1)

Create bespoke, editorial-grade HD headline lettering on a genuinely TRANSPARENT
background: no scenery outside the letters, no paragraphs, no buttons, no labels,
no paper or plate. Use the reference only for the exact wording and the Pardus
palette (midnight navy, cobalt, champagne gold).

Exact text, nothing else: line one "A world of places." and line two "A journey
of your own."

Direction: the confidence of a luxury-house masthead, not a poster. Draw a
high-contrast display serif with sharp, tapered hairlines and sturdy stems,
tightly and optically kerned. "A world of" in deep navy at heavy weight. "places."
as a single flowing cobalt italic with a long, confident entry stroke and a
generous swash on the final full stop, connected to "of" so the two read as one
lockup. Contained inside the thick strokes of "places" only, show sunlit
Mediterranean terraces, turquoise water and a sliver of palm coastline, cut cleanly
to the letter shapes. Leave the outer 2 px of every contour as solid navy or
cobalt so the word stays crisp on any background.

"A journey of your own." sits beneath in a refined bronze-gold italic of the same
family, smaller and noticeably lighter, with open letter-spacing, a subtle
metallic gradient (deep bronze to warm champagne) and no glow.

Finishing: one tiny gold compass star inside a letter counter and one fine gold
flight-path line that leaves the final stop of "places." and curls out of the
lockup. Both restrained. Perfectly smooth vector-quality edges, zero colour
fringing, no drop shadow, no 3D bevel, no outer glow, no noise, no texture on the
letter edges, no semi-transparent haze around the letters. Safe transparent
margin all round. Return only the finished transparent headline.

## Phone (about 1.5 : 1, shown at about 330 CSS px wide)

Same identity, re-composed for a phone, not shrunk: line one "A world of", line
two "places." much larger, then the gold italic in two balanced lines "A journey
of" / "your own." Make the navy and cobalt letters bolder and the hairlines a
little heavier so every stroke survives at 330 px. Keep the photographic
Mediterranean inside "places." simple and large-scale (two or three clear zones,
not fine detail). One small gold compass star, one small gold plane at the
terminal. Transparent background, solid crisp edges, no glow, no shadow, no
halo. Return only the transparent mobile headline.

## If the first result is not right, add one of these

- More power: "Increase the stroke weight of the navy serif by about 15 percent and
  tighten the line spacing so the lockup feels dense and commanding."
- More refinement: "Reduce the gold phrase to 70 percent of its size, increase its
  letter-spacing, and remove every ornament except the compass star."
- Edge quality: "Redraw every letter edge as clean vector curves; remove any soft,
  blurred or speckled pixels along the letter outlines."
