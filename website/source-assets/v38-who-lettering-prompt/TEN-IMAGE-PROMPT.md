# Ten matching card and section photographs (V38)

Shaped for the tighter V38 layout: cards now show a wide 16:10 picture (16:9 on
phones, 16:11 in the destination grid), so every image is composed wide with the
subject in the middle 60 percent of the frame and nothing important near the top or
bottom edge. Photographic, calm, premium, no text.

## One prompt that makes all ten

Create a set of TEN different, matching luxury travel photographs, delivered as ten
separate images (not a collage, not a grid). Every image: landscape 16:10, very high
resolution, photorealistic editorial photography, natural light, true-to-life
colour, shallow haze in the distance, no people facing the camera, no faces in
focus, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no borders, no frames, no UI, no
illustration or CGI look, no oversaturated HDR. Shared grade: deep teal and
midnight-navy shadows, warm champagne highlights, a restrained touch of brass-gold
in the light, gentle contrast. Composition: the subject sits in the middle 60
percent of the frame; the top 15 and bottom 15 percent hold only sky, water or
ground so the picture can be cropped to 16:9 without losing anything. Calm and
uncluttered, one clear focal point per image.

The ten images, in order:
1. Maldives: a long timber jetty leading to overwater villas over turquoise
   shallows, late-morning light, one white sandbank in the distance.
2. Seychelles: giant smooth granite boulders on a pale beach, palms behind,
   clear emerald water, soft cloud.
3. Dubai: the skyline at golden hour seen across still water from a marina
   promenade, glass towers glowing, one slim tower dominant.
4. East Africa safari: a small family of elephants crossing open golden grassland,
   acacia silhouettes, low warm sun, long soft shadows.
5. Europe: a whitewashed Mediterranean cliffside town above deep blue sea,
   terraced stairs, a single sailboat, clear afternoon.
6. Zanzibar: an aerial view of a pale beach and shallow green-turquoise lagoon,
   a traditional white-sailed dhow, a few palms at the edge.
7. Private yacht: a white motor yacht at anchor off a coast at sunset, calm
   water, warm reflected light, no one on the deck in focus.
8. Executive arrival: a quiet private-terminal apron at dawn with a business jet
   and a dark car waiting, cool blue light with a thin band of gold on the horizon.
9. Private island reset: a bare sand-floor beachfront villa terrace with linen
   daybeds facing a calm sea, early light, absolutely still.
10. Celebration evening: a long table set for a private dinner on a beach at
    dusk, lanterns, low flames, ocean behind, empty chairs, no guests in focus.

Keep the same lens look (35 to 50 mm equivalent), the same grade and the same calm
mood across all ten so they sit together as one collection. Return the ten
images only.

## If you can only make one image per run

Use the same text, replacing the list with one numbered line, and begin with:
"This is image N of a matching set of ten; keep the set's shared grade and
composition rules."
